### 🔬 Research Interests

**Trustworthy evaluation of clinical prediction models**
<br><sub>When labels in health records are missing or selectively reported, a model's reported performance can depend more on assumptions than on data. I study which conclusions about a model actually hold, especially for calibration and transfer across hospitals.</sub>

**Privacy-preserving and distributed learning for healthcare**
<br><sub>Split learning and federated learning for data that cannot leave the hospital or the device, and the trade-offs between privacy, communication cost and model performance.</sub>

**Efficient deep learning for real-world deployment**
<br><sub>Lightweight models and careful training that run on limited hardware without giving up reliability.</sub>

### 🌱 Long-term Vision

<sub>I want medical AI that clinicians can trust: models whose limits are stated as clearly as their accuracy, that respect patient privacy, and that keep working when they move from the hospital where they were built to the next one. I hope to pursue a PhD on building and evaluating such systems.</sub>
